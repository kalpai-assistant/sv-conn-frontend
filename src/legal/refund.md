# Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Booking Cancellation

1.1 Customer Cancellation

- Customers may cancel a booking free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled service time.
- Cancellations made within 2 hours of the scheduled service may attract a cancellation fee.

  1.2 Professional Already Assigned or Arrived

- If the service professional has already reached the customer's location or is end route, a visit/inspection charge may be applicable.
- No refund shall be provided for completed visits or inspections.

2. Rescheduling

- Customers may reschedule a booking without charge if requested at least 2 hours before the scheduled service time.
- Multiple rescheduling requests may be subject to additional charges.

3. Refund Policy

3.1 Full Refund A full refund will be issued when:

- The service is cancelled by the company.
- The service cannot be delivered due to operational reasons attributable to the company.
- Payment has been charged erroneously.

  3.2 Partial Refund A partial refund may be issued after deducting applicable cancellation, visit, inspection, or administrative charges.

  3.3 No Refund Refunds will not be provided:

- After successful completion of the service.
- For customer no-shows.
- If incorrect address or contact details are provided.
- For dissatisfaction where the service has been delivered as agreed.

4. Service Quality Issues If a customer is dissatisfied with the service:

- The company may offer a free revisit or corrective service.
- Refund requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

5. Spare Parts & Materials

- Charges for installed spare parts and materials are non-refundable.
- Special-order parts may be subject to supplier cancellation charges.

6. Refund Processing

- Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method, UPI, bank transfer, or company wallet.
- Refunds are generally processed within 5-7 working days.

7. Company Cancellation The company reserves the right to cancel or reschedule bookings due to:

- Professional unavailability.
- Safety concerns.
- Force majeure events.
- Operational constraints. In such cases, customers will receive a full refund of any prepaid amount.
